We thank Reviewer QHmk for the thorough review. We address each concern below.

**W1: RL contextualization.** We agree and will revise. Our setting is reward-proportional posterior sampling within the broader MaxEnt/KL-regularized RL family [Tiapkin et al., 2024; Deleu et al., 2024]. The introduction's "in contrast to reward-maximizing RL" overstates the distinction and will be corrected. Our contribution is specifically about balance-based training objectives on terminable prefix trees, which applies regardless of whether one views GFlowNets as a standalone framework or as a special case of entropy-regularized RL.

**W2: PPO/GRPO baselines.** We evaluate both on Expr24 and SMILES with identical model/LoRA/budget (unconstrained generation with soft vocab masking, following the standard RL paradigm in REINVENT/PSV-PPO). On SMILES (dense QED reward), GRPO achieves QED=0.661 but entropy collapses to 0.98 (vs RapTB+SubM's 2.726 — only 36% diversity). PPO collapses further to entropy=0.00 (generating a single molecule). On Expr24 (sparse reward), GRPO learns reward=0.87 but with similarly reduced diversity; PPO never finds a valid expression. Critically, the diversity gap persists even on the dense-reward SMILES task, confirming the issue is reward-maximizing vs distributional objectives, not reward sparsity.

**W3: TBA baseline.** TBA [Bartoldson et al., 2025] is the most relevant system-level related work. Its core contribution is asynchronous search, prioritized replay, and distributed TB training at 410M-7B scale. Our contribution is orthogonal: improving credit assignment and termination calibration within the TB objective. RapTB could serve as a drop-in replacement for the TB loss inside TBA's training loop. We will add explicit TBA discussion in related work and framing.

**W4/W5: Mathematical explanation and terminology.** We expand the key design rationale:
- The learnable Z is NOT removed. It remains in the terminal TB anchor (Eq. 9). The rooted residual (Eq. 4) cancels Z only in the auxiliary branch, creating a root-anchored local consistency signal that avoids O(N) redundant optimization of the same global scalar.
- The absorbed suffix backup (Eqs. 5-7) reduces early-prefix target variance: u_k^max provides a continuation lower-bound, u_k^soft smoothly aggregates suffix evidence, and alpha interpolates between them. Table 6 confirms that neither endpoint alone matches the mixed target.
- We will define "termination drift" at first mention (Section 1) and forward-reference Appendix C.6.

**Q1: Global optimum guarantee.** No new exact guarantee is claimed for the full composite RapTB objective. The exact reward-proportional fixed point remains tied to the terminal TB anchor; the auxiliary branch is introduced as a variance-reducing optimization aid. This is stated in Eq. (9): "The TB term remains the only exact balance condition."

**Q2: Averaged-prefix TB baseline.** We implement and evaluate exactly this baseline: AvgPrefixTB averages (Delta_k^TB)^2 over all k in [0, tau] with a learnable log Z.

Expr24 results (6400 samples, RP replay):

| Method | Acc | Unique_valid | NormCov | KL(pi->p*) | JS | log p_term |
|--------|-----|-------------|---------|------------|------|-----------|
| AvgPrefixTB | 0.998 | 142 | 0.016 | 0.808 | 0.213 | -0.560 |
| RapTB | 0.991 | 246.7 | 0.039 | 0.561 | 0.147 | -0.065 |
| TB | 1.000 | 5.3 | 0.001 | 1.297 | 0.339 | -0.001 |

AvgPrefixTB achieves near-perfect accuracy (0.998) but severe mode collapse: only 142 unique valid expressions vs RapTB's 247, NormCov 0.016 vs 0.039, and worse distributional fidelity (JS 0.213 vs 0.147). Its termination calibration (log p_term = -0.560) is notably worse than RapTB's (-0.065), indicating partial termination suppression consistent with our diagnosis. Under Oracle replay, AvgPrefixTB reaches NormCov 0.183 (vs RapTB 0.816), confirming the gap persists even with controlled coverage.

On SMILES (L_max=10), the distinction is even starker: AvgPrefixTB achieves Acc=1.000 but QED=0.661, Diversity=0.665, FPDiv=0.649 — all the worst among all methods. Mean length 2.89 (54% at L=1-2) indicates severe short-sequence collapse. By contrast, RapTB+SubM achieves QED=0.844, Diversity=2.726 at comparable validity.

These results confirm that simple prefix averaging does NOT match RapTB. The gains come specifically from: (i) rooted residuals that cancel Z in the auxiliary branch, (ii) absorbed suffix backups that provide lower-variance targets, and (iii) stop-gradient on the termination head that prevents drift.

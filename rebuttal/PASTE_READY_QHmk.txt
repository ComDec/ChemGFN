We thank Reviewer QHmk for the thorough review. We address each concern below.

**W1: RL contextualization.** We agree and will revise. Our setting is reward-proportional posterior sampling within the broader MaxEnt/KL-regularized RL family [Tiapkin et al., 2024; Deleu et al., 2024]. The introduction's "in contrast to reward-maximizing RL" overstates the distinction and will be corrected. Our contribution is specifically about balance-based training objectives on terminable prefix trees, which applies regardless of whether one views GFlowNets as a standalone framework or as a special case of entropy-regularized RL.

**W2: PPO/GRPO baselines.** We agree these have value as reward-maximization references. Our primary comparison targets the TB-family because we modify the TB objective itself; PPO/GRPO solve a different problem (mode-seeking reward maximization vs. reward-proportional sampling). We will clarify this distinction and add a qualitative positioning in the revision. A fair quantitative comparison requires matching the full training pipeline and is best addressed in follow-up work.

**W3: TBA baseline.** TBA [Bartoldson et al., 2025] is the most relevant system-level related work. Its core contribution is asynchronous search, prioritized replay, and distributed TB training at 410M-7B scale. Our contribution is orthogonal: improving credit assignment and termination calibration within the TB objective. RapTB could serve as a drop-in replacement for the TB loss inside TBA's training loop. We will add explicit TBA discussion in related work and framing.

**W4/W5: Mathematical explanation and terminology.** We expand the key design rationale:
- The learnable Z is NOT removed. It remains in the terminal TB anchor (Eq. 9). The rooted residual (Eq. 4) cancels Z only in the auxiliary branch, creating a root-anchored local consistency signal that avoids O(N) redundant optimization of the same global scalar.
- The absorbed suffix backup (Eqs. 5-7) reduces early-prefix target variance: u_k^max provides a continuation lower-bound, u_k^soft smoothly aggregates suffix evidence, and alpha interpolates between them. Table 6 confirms that neither endpoint alone matches the mixed target.
- We will define "termination drift" at first mention (Section 1) and forward-reference Appendix C.6.

**Q1: Global optimum guarantee.** No new exact guarantee is claimed for the full composite RapTB objective. The exact reward-proportional fixed point remains tied to the terminal TB anchor; the auxiliary branch is introduced as a variance-reducing optimization aid. This is stated in Eq. (9): "The TB term remains the only exact balance condition."

**Q2: Averaged-prefix TB baseline.** We implement and evaluate exactly this baseline. We test three variants:
(a) AvgPrefixTB: average (Delta_k^TB)^2 over all k in [0, tau] — the strict version of your suggestion.
(b) AvgPrefixTB + detach pterm: same, but stop-gradient on log p_term in the auxiliary branch.
(c) TB + AvgAux: terminal TB anchor + averaged prefix auxiliary (controlled comparison with RapTB).

We thank all reviewers for their careful and constructive feedback. Before responding individually, we address three overarching themes and summarize new experiments conducted during the rebuttal period.

**1. RapTB preserves the reward-proportional optimum (Pd1v-W3, JxzD-W1, QHmk-Q1).**

We provide a two-angle analysis showing that the auxiliary term does not move the optimum away from the reward-proportional solution.

*Angle 1 (structure).* RapTB decomposes as TB + weighted auxiliary regularizer (Eq. 9). The TB fixed point, established by Malkin et al. [1, Theorem 1] and shown equivalent to the MaxEnt RL optimal policy by Tiapkin et al. [2, Theorem 1], is the sole exact balance condition. The auxiliary is controlled by η and does not introduce a new fixed point, structurally paralleling how FL-GFN [8] and LED-GFN [9] add reparameterizations/regularizers while preserving the TB fixed point.

*Angle 2 (zero-at-optimum).* In the terminable prefix tree, the TB residual at prefix k is:

  Δ_k^TB(ξ) = log Z_θ + Σ_{t<k} log P_F(s_{t+1}|s_{0:t}) + log P_F(⊤|s_{0:k}) − log R(s_{0:k}^⊤)

When the TB condition holds for all termination points (Δ_k^TB = 0 for all k), the rooted residuals cancel: Δ̄_k = Δ_k^TB − Δ_0^TB = 0. The auxiliary loss then vanishes:

  L_aux(ξ) = Σ_k w_k · (Δ̄_k + u_k − u_k^tgt)² / Σ_k w_k = 0

Therefore L_RapTB = (Δ^TB)² + η · L_aux = 0 + 0 = 0 at the reward-proportional solution. Both terms share the same global minimum. This parallels SubTB's same-fixed-point property via telescoping [6]. We emphasize this is algebraic consistency — the two loss terms are jointly minimized at the same point — not a convergence theorem.

**2. RL contextualization and TBA (QHmk-W1/W3, Pd1v-W2).**

We agree that GFlowNets can be reformulated as entropy-regularized RL [2, 3], with SubTB equivalent to Path Consistency Learning [5]. Our contribution is narrower: within off-policy TB-family training on terminable prefix trees, we improve prefix-level credit assignment. This applies regardless of whether one views the problem through the GFlowNet or the entropy-regularized RL lens.

Regarding TBA [7]: TBA is a system-level pipeline for asynchronous TB-based LLM post-training. TBA explicitly acknowledges TB's gradient variance as a limitation and suggests "learning partial energy functions to balance bias and variance" as future work. RapTB's absorbed suffix backups address this gap as a non-parametric realization of partial energy functions without auxiliary networks (cf. FL-GFN [8], which requires intermediate energy evaluable at every state, and LED-GFN [9], which requires a separate potential network). RapTB can serve as a drop-in objective for TBA's training loop. We will revise the introduction to properly contextualize within the RL literature.

**3. RapTB + SubM complementarity (cA3o-Q1, Pd1v-W2).**

SubM drives external mode discovery; RapTB improves internal credit allocation. The two operate on orthogonal axes and combine additively. Three regimes demonstrate this:

| Regime | Key Comparison | Result |
|--------|---------------|--------|
| RP replay | RapTB vs TB | RapTB improves coverage (NormCov 0.039 vs 0.001; JS 0.147 vs 0.339) |
| SubM replay | RapTB+SubM vs TB+SubM | RapTB+SubM doubles NormCov (0.209 vs 0.100) |
| Oracle replay | RapTB vs TB (controlled coverage) | RapTB improves Acc (0.945 vs 0.919) and both KL directions |

Under controlled exploration (Oracle), the objective-level improvement is isolated from the replay effect, confirming that RapTB's credit assignment contributes independently.

**4. New experiments summary.**

| Experiment | Setup | Key Result |
|------------|-------|------------|
| PPO/GRPO baselines | Same model/LoRA/budget, Expr24 + SMILES | GRPO Expr24: Acc=0.002 (12/6400 valid, 1 unique); GRPO SMILES: entropy=0.98 (36% of GFlowNet's 2.726). PPO: crash at step 250, entropy→0. |
| AvgPrefixTB | Average (Δ_k^TB)² over all k with learnable Z | SMILES: Diversity=0.665, Len=2.89 (collapse); Expr24: NormCov=0.016 vs RapTB's 0.039 |
| β×ρ sweep (18 configs) | 9 Expr24 + 9 SMILES, full 5K-step training | Expr24: all 9 Acc≥0.983, log p_term∈[−0.25,−0.04]; SMILES: 8/9 Acc≥0.991, FPDiv 0.849–0.883 |
| 3B scale-up | Llama-3.2-3B LoRA on SMILES | SubTB Acc=0.313 (drift worsens); RapTB+SubM: Acc=0.996, QED=0.856, FPDiv=0.937 |
| AMP biological sequences | 20-aa vocab, non-differentiable fitness, 3K steps | SubTB length collapse (Len=49.3); RapTB+SubM best diversity (16.92) / novelty (15.77) among natural-length methods |

The AvgPrefixTB result is informative: simple dense prefix supervision collapses to short sequences on both tasks. The gain from RapTB is not "more prefix objectives" but specifically: (1) rooted Z-cancellation that decouples prefix updates from the global partition function, (2) suffix-absorbed targets that provide non-parametric partial energy signals, and (3) stop-gradient on the auxiliary termination head that prevents the drift diagnosed in Appendix C.6.

**Claim scope revision.** In light of these discussions, we narrow the claim from "general LLM-GFlowNet training" to "terminable prefix-tree LLM-GFlowNets in the evaluated settings." We now cover molecules (SMILES), arithmetic (Expr24), natural language (CommonGen), and biological sequences (AMP), at both 1B and 3B scale, but do not yet establish results on code or mathematical reasoning tasks.

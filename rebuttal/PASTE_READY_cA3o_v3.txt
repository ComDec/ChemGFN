**W2+Q1: When does RapTB provide additive benefit over SubM?** SubM and RapTB address orthogonal bottlenecks: SubM improves external replay coverage; RapTB improves internal credit allocation. We isolate their contributions in three controlled regimes on Expr24:

| Regime | TB metric | RapTB metric | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| RP replay (no SubM) | NormCov 0.001 | NormCov 0.039 | RapTB alone improves 39× |
| SubM replay | NormCov 0.100 | NormCov 0.209 | RapTB doubles SubM's benefit |
| Oracle replay (controlled) | Acc 0.919, JS 0.016 | Acc 0.945, JS 0.013 | Better allocation, same data |

Under RP (no SubM), exploration is the dominant bottleneck and even 39× improvement in NormCov remains modest. Once SubM provides adequate coverage, RapTB doubles NormCov (0.209 vs 0.100). Under Oracle replay where coverage is equalized, RapTB still improves Acc and both KL directions — this isolates the allocation benefit. On SMILES, RapTB+SubM shifts more probability mass to the longest bin (Frac[9-10]: 0.402 vs 0.323 for TB+SubM), confirming better credit assignment to hard-to-reach regions.

**Q4: GAE analogy.** The connection is a structural analogy in variance reduction, not an equivalence. Three key differences prevent a direct mapping:

(i) *Optimization target.* GAE [6] estimates advantages for policy gradient ∇J. RapTB constructs targets for squared balance residuals (L² loss) — different optimization geometry.

(ii) *Value function.* GAE bootstraps from a learned V(s), controlling bias-variance via λ. In the entropy-regularized RL view [1], log F(s) = V*(s) (soft value function). FL-GFN [3] reparameterizes via intermediate energy E(s) but requires E(s) evaluable at every state (Assumption 4.1 — violated under sparse rewards). LED-GFN [4] learns potentials with an auxiliary network. RapTB uses non-parametric suffix aggregation — no learned value function, no auxiliary model. TBA [5] explicitly calls for "learning partial energy functions" as future work; our absorbed targets are a computationally cheap realization.

(iii) *Global constraint.* The GFlowNet TB condition imposes a partition function constraint absent in standard policy gradient.

We will revise the discussion to connect explicitly to the RL view and clarify the claim scope to "evaluated settings."
